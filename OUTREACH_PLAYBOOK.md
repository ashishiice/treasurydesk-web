# TreasuryDesk — NBFC Outreach Playbook

Prepared: 06 Aug 2026 · Companion to the engagement site (https://ashishiice.github.io/treasurydesk-web/)

## 1. Target selection logic

Sweet spot: NBFCs / HFCs with AUM ~₹500–2,500 Cr
- Too small (<₹300 Cr): no budget, treasury = CFO's side task
- Too big (>₹5,000 Cr): have in-house desks (Shriram, Tata Capital, etc.)
- Right size: actively raising (CP/NCD pipeline), thin treasury teams, regulated ALM/liquidity obligations, no automation

Also target: companies that JUST crossed into RBI's Middle Layer (NBFC-ML) — new ALM/liquidity/CRAR reporting obligations create immediate treasury pain.

## 3. Contact channel playbook

Priority order per company:
1. **LinkedIn — Head of Treasury / CFO** (best: warm intro from mutual connection in BFSI)
2. **Investor relations email** (works well for listed cos: Aptus, Muthoot Microfin, U Gro)
3. **Company contact form** (lowest conversion but zero cost)
4. **FIDC events / webinars** (in-person > cold email; FIDC is the industry body — attend 1-2 events, target treasurer talks)

## 4. Sales process

1. Week 1-2: LinkedIn + email outreach, 10 companies/day (Tier 1 = 6, then Tier 2)
2. Week 2-3: 30-min walkthroughs (show live demos, map their funding mix)
3. Week 3-4: 5-day free pilot (full output, zero obligation)
4. Week 4-6: close first 2-3 clients at ₹50k-1L/month
5. Iterate: use pilot feedback to sharpen the offer

## 5. Pricing recap (from site)

- Essentials ₹50k/mo — briefs + alerts + T-bill monitor + weekly funding summary
- Professional ₹1L/mo — + funding optimizer + liquidity pulse + EOD wrap + quarterly ALM memo
- Enterprise custom — multi-entity, custom connectors, dedicated fractional treasurer
