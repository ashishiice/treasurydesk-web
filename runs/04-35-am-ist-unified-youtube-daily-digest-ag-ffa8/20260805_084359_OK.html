He notes the drone restriction triggered dramatic investment into drone supply chain, and they expect the same for robotics. **The Elon growth thesis and speed-of-change (mid-section):** Diamandis cites Elon's prediction of double-digit GDP growth in 18-24 months, triple-digit growth in 5 years, and a post-capitalist 2036 where "anything you could possibly want will be delivered by AI and robotics." **Education for the AI era (55:30–60:45):** The education system needs fundamental rethinking for an AI-native era — not incremental curriculum adjustment but reimagining what skills matter when AI handles cognitive tasks.

**Vision for accelerated scientific discovery (60:45–end):** Kratsios references Demis Hassabis telling him the goal is to "cure all diseases in 10 years." The closing frames the golden age as achievable if funding, regulatory, and incentive structures align with AI-native research processes.
