## Pitfalls
1. **Gold futures contract price confusion**: `GC=F` returns the COMEX gold futures **contract** price (~$4,600–4,800 for 100 troy oz), NOT the per-ounce spot price (~$2,300–2,500/oz). When reporting gold, always divide `GC=F` by 100 to express per-ounce spot, or explicitly label the raw figure as "Gold futures contract". Never present the raw contract price as the spot price — it will be misread as a massive move.
2. **bot_detection**: Yahoo Finance / Investing.com may block with bot detection. Use snapshots not raw curl. If blocked, try CNBC or DuckDuckGo HTML search as fallback.
2. **vision_analyze failure**: On OpenRouter key limit exceeded (403), browser_vision will fail. Fall back to browser_snapshot.
3. **Timing mismatch**: US markets close at 4 PM ET (1:30 AM IST). At 9 PM IST they're still open ~2.5 hours. Label data correctly.
4. **Asia holiday closures**: Check CNBC/HKEX/TSE calendars. Many Asia markets closed for Easter/Qingming/Golden Week. Always note which markets are closed.
5. **Geopolitical events dominate**: If active US-Iran conflict or similar, this overrides ALL suppression rules. Always report escalation timelines, deadlines, and Oval Office press conferences.
6. **Link timeouts on CNBC**: Clicking links on CNBC pages can timeout. If `browser_click` fails, use `browser_navigate` directly to the article URL instead.
7. **Commodities table scrolling unreliable**: Yahoo Finance commodities page tables require multiple scrolls and may truncate. Direct quote pages (CL%3DF, BZ%3DF, etc.) load instantly with full data.
8. **Treasury yields on Yahoo bonds page**: The bonds overview page lists futures prices, not yields. Use CNBC quote pages (US2Y, US10Y) for actual yield data with "KEY STATS" section.
9. **Fast-moving geopolitical stories**: During active conflicts, check both Yahoo quote page news sections AND CNBC trending topics. Critical updates appear in both places.
10. **Yield change calculation**: CNBC shows both current yield and previous close in KEY STATS. Calculate change as (Current - Previous Close). The page also shows change in the header but verify against KEY STATS for accuracy.
11. **Yahoo API vs CNBC yield discrepancy**: Yahoo `^TNX`, `^TYX`, `^FVX`, `^IRX` return futures index prices, NOT actual Treasury yields in %. Always supplement with CNBC quote pages (US2Y, US10Y, US30Y) for true yield percentages. Convert CNBC yield change from percentage points to bps (×100).
12. **KOSPI holiday gaps**: KOSPI closes can gap >5% across holidays (May 1, Chuseok, Seollal). If you see an extreme single-day move, verify whether Korean markets were closed the prior trading day. Use `range=5d` on the Yahoo API to inspect multi-day closes for context.
13. **delegate_task failure on market data**: `delegate_task` with web-search subagents frequently fails for real-time market data due to model tool-call loops (timeouts at 600s with stuck API calls). Use direct `execute_code` + Yahoo API or `browser_navigate` + `browser_snapshot` instead. **Also note: even parallel batches of 3 subagents timed out for this task** — the cron `max_concurrent_children` cap is 3, but for real-time market data a single subagent can easily exceed the 10-minute timeout. Skip `delegate_task` entirely for time-sensitive market scans.
14. **Asian indices `regularMarketPrice` is after-hours futures, not last cash close**: For `^KS11`, `^HSI`, `^N225`, Yahoo's `meta.regularMarketPrice` returned OUTSIDE Asian trading hours reflects overnight after-hours futures (e.g., KOSPI showed 7484.41 vs actual last cash close 8160.59 — a fake -11.7% move). **Always use the last entry in `indicators.quote[0].close` and compare to the prior entry, NOT `meta.regularMarketPrice` and `meta.chartPreviousClose`, for Asian indices.** See `references/market-data-verification.md` for the general verification pattern; treat Asian indices as a special case where `meta` fields are unreliable outside Asian session hours.
15. **Yahoo `range=2d` returns 2 data points; for week-over-week context use `range=5d`**: When computing "this week vs last week" changes (e.g., "10Y +9.7bp this week"), 2-day data misses the trend. Use `range=5d` and compare the last close to a 5-day-ago close.
16. **Delisted/missing Yahoo symbols return 108-byte error JSON**: A symbol like `TWV` or `HSI=F` returns `{"chart":{"result":null,"error":{"code":"Not Found","description":"No data found, symbol may be delisted"}}}` with HTTP 200. Always check `len(response) > 500` before parsing; if response is <500 bytes, treat the symbol as failed and skip it (don't crash the JSON parser).
17. **`2YY=F` returns futures, with sparse history**: The `2YY=F` symbol returned only ~3 days of close data on `range=5d` queries while other Yahoo Treasury symbols return 5 days. Not a data error — the contract is near expiration. Use the most recent value as the "current" 2Y yield proxy and label clearly as "CME 2Y yield futures" not "US 2Y Treasury yield."
19. **Cron job timing: 9 PM IST is 11:30 AM ET — US markets are MID-SESSION**: When this cron fires on a weekday, the most recent US CLOSE was yesterday (1:30 AM IST), and US is currently in mid-session. Label data as "Live [time] ET / [time] IST" and footnote that the official close is at 1:30 AM IST. If the user's template column says "Close", populate it with the live snapshot and footnote the market status rather than waiting for a close that happens after Asian markets open.
20. **`NKD=F` Nikkei futures can spike on contract roll**: This session returned 71,935 vs a prior 65,570 (+9.7%) while the Nikkei cash close was +1.65%. For Asian open indication, prefer `^N225` spot (verified against the CNBC world banner) over `NKD=F`.
21. **AP News hubs are bot-resistant**: When Reuters/CNN/Bloomberg block or require CAPTCHA, AP News topic hubs (`/hub/russia-ukraine`, `/hub/china`, `/hub/economy`) load cleanly and provide headline-level geopolitical/macro coverage.
22. **Federal Reserve calendar pages load via JS**: The official Fed speeches/press-release pages often show "Loading data..." in the browser snapshot. Use the RSS feeds (`/feeds/press_all.xml`, `/feeds/speeches.xml`) for reliable, parseable Fed statements and speaker schedules.
