- **Scale read:** This is the largest notified VRRR of the current cycle (state shows 7-day ₹6L cr Sep 01, 3-day ₹7L cr Sep 04, overnight + multi-tranche dailies through the week). Stacking ₹7L cr overnight/3-day + now a 30-day lock signals RBI deliberately converting transient surplus into a month-long absorbed balance — consistent with net durable liquidity of +₹8.06L cr (Aug 15 print in MMO) being managed down ahead of festival-season currency leakage and a heavy H2 borrowing calendar.
- **Cross-sector:** Banks place surplus at ~5.24% risk-free, narrowing the carry advantage of deploying into CP/NCD/T-bill funding plays — mild upward pressure at the short end of the money-market curve. NBFC-ML read: CP investors' cash gets a government floor at 5.24%, so short CP pricing has to clear above that; rollover spreads tighten only if absorption keeps rates elevated rather than scarce.
- **What the text does not say:** No stated terminal stock target, no OMO signal, no indication this pre-announces CRR/lever management. Treat as liquidity fine-tuning, not a stance change (stance remains neutral per last MPC).
- **Watch:** Fill rate on Sep 07 — if participation <70%, banks are preferring the overnight route and the 30-day tool may need re-pricing; premature-reversal volume is the new telemetry RBI has given itself.
