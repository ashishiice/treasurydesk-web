When running as a scheduled cron job, `execute_code` may be blocked because it runs arbitrary local Python without user approval. **Do not rely on `execute_code` for scan orchestration in cron mode.** Instead:

## Announcement Interpretation Rules (Critical — Added 2026-06-09)
When scanning the NSE announcements feed, **filter description text for the following keywords and treat them per the rules below — these distinguish real catalysts from noise/negative flags:**

| Description (exact) | Interpretation | Action |
|---|---|---|
| "Outcome of Board Meeting" | Real catalyst (results, dividend, fundraising, M&A) | HIGH score (Earnings_catalyst = 9-10) |
| "Change in Management" | Neutral-to-positive (new leadership) — but verify direction | MEDIUM, only if new appointment, not director death |
| "Change in Management - Demise of …" | **NEGATIVE** | EXCLUDE (downgrade Earnings_catalyst to 0; this is a sympathy bounce, not a catalyst) |
| "Price movement" | **WARNING FLAG** — NSE asked company to clarify; "response is awaited" means no positive catalyst disclosed | EXCLUDE or treat as 0; the NSE flag itself is bearish/cautionary, not a buy signal |
| "Spurt in Volume" | Volume anomaly, not a fundamental catalyst | Use to **augment Volume_spike** score; do NOT count as Earnings_catalyst |
| "Disclosure under SEBI Takeover Regulations" | **Conditional HIGH** — only if it is a fresh open offer / Regulation 29 stake change or other material acquisition/disposal. Routine annual promoter holding declarations under Regulation 31(4) are neutral. | HIGH only after verification; otherwise LOW/0 |
| "Voluntary Delisting" | EXCLUDE — imminent exit, not a buy | EXCLUDE |
| "Analysts/Institutional Investor Meet/Con. Call Updates" | Often routine, not material | LOW unless specific company name on the call list |
| "ESOP/ESOS/ESPS" | Employee scheme — neutral-to-mildly-bullish (alignment) | LOW (use only if company is small and ESOP is material %) |
| "Defaults on Payment of Interest/Principal" | **NEGATIVE** — company defaulted on debt | EXCLUDE (Earnings_catalyst = 0; this is a credit event, not a buy signal). Added 2026-06-17 after RKEC scan. |
| "Stock split" | NEUTRAL-to-CAUTION — corporate action, not a fundamental catalyst | LOW; stock splits in microcaps can be dilutive or signal retail-appeal manipulation. Do NOT score as Earnings_catalyst. |
| "Credit Rating- Others" | NEUTRAL — rating action, verify direction | LOW unless upgrade; downgrade = NEGATIVE |
| "General Updates" / "Updates" | Usually boilerplate | LOW — read attachment text before scoring; sometimes hides material info (e.g. warrant conversion, project updates) |
| "Bagging/Receiving of orders/contracts" | **MEDIUM-HIGH** — order win, material for microcap revenue | MEDIUM (score_boost = 6); read attachment for order size. Added 2026-07-08. |
| "Change in Company Secretary/Compliance Officer" | Routine compliance | EXCLUDE (score_boost = 0). Added 2026-07-08. |
| "Structural Digital Database" | SDD compliance certificate | EXCLUDE (score_boost = 0). Added 2026-07-08. |
| "Certificate under SEBI (Depositories and Participants) Regulations, 2018" | Reg 74(5) compliance | EXCLUDE (score_boost = 0). Added 2026-07-08. |
| "Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process" | **NEGATIVE** — company is in bankruptcy/insolvency proceedings under IBC | EXCLUDE (Earnings_catalyst = 0; score_boost = 0). This is a credit/distress event, not a buy signal. Added 2026-07-13 after ANSALAPI and SABEVENTS filings. |
| "Cessation" | **NEGATIVE-to-NEUTRAL** — director resignation/departure | LOW/EXCLUDE (score_boost = 0-1). Cessation of a director is usually routine resignation but can signal internal turmoil in microcaps. Do NOT score as catalyst. Added 2026-07-13 after WINSOME filing. |

**Updates/General Updates material-content pitfall (added after 2026-06-22 scan):** NSE filings labeled merely "Updates" or "General Updates" frequently contain **material substance** in the `attchmntText` field even though the `desc` field is boilerplate. Example: MSP Steel & Power (MSPL) filed "Updates" on 20-Jun-2026 with `attchmntText`: *"Open Market Purchase of Equity Shares by Promoter Entity"* — a clear smart-money/promoter-confidence signal. **Rule:** Always inspect `attchmntText` (or download the PDF) for any "Updates" / "General Updates" filing before assigning a LOW score. If the attachment reveals promoter buying, capacity expansion, or new orders, upgrade appropriately.

**Updates-with-board-outcome rule (added after 2026-06-23 scan):** If an "Updates" filing's `attchmntText` references audited quarterly/annual financial results, an "Outcome of Board Meeting," or a board meeting that approved results/dividend/fundraising, treat it exactly as an "Outcome of Board Meeting" HIGH catalyst. Example: MOHITIND (19-Jun-2026) filed under "Updates" but the attachment said *"Submission of Machine-Readable Form and Legible copies of the Audited Standalone and Consolidated Financial Results for the quarter and financial year ended March 31, 2026, approved at the Board Meeting ... held on May 29, 2026."* This is a real earnings catalyst, not boilerplate.

**Takeover-disclosure verification pitfall (added after 2026-06-19 scan):** `Disclosure under SEBI Takeover Regulations` is **not automatically a fresh catalyst**. Many filings are annual promoter/promoter-group declarations under Regulation 31(4), e.g. “as on March 31, 2026, we collectively hold …” with no new acquisition/disposal. Treat those as neutral (`Earnings_catalyst = 0` or LOW) unless the text/PDF shows a fresh `Regulation 29`, `open offer`, `acquired`, `disposed`, `change in shareholding`, or similar stake/voting-right event.

**Price-movement flag pitfall (added after 2026-06-09 scan):** NSE filing "Price movement / Significant movement in price has been observed in X. The Exchange has written to the company. The response is awaited." is **NOT** an unpriced positive catalyst. It is the regulator asking the company to explain unusual price action. Most often this flags potential price manipulation, a pre-result build-up, or insider trading. Treat as `Earnings_catalyst = 0`. Do not let the +20% intraday move fool you — that's the move the flag is warning about.

## Fundamental-Data Fallback — Screener.in Company Pages (Added 2026-06-15)
Yahoo v8 gives price, volume and 5-day closes but **not market cap or PE**. For penny-stock filtering (mcap < ₹500 Cr, PE < 25), use Screener.in company pages as a curl-parsable fallback.

- URL pattern: `https://www.screener.in/company/{SYMBOL}/` (e.g. `https://www.screener.in/company/KELLTONTEC/`).
- The page returns 200 for valid NSE symbols and contains `Current Price`, `Market Cap`, `Stock P/E` in a predictable HTML structure.
- Some values are split, e.g. Current Price shows `₹ 33.2 / 13.0` (current/consolidated). Always take the **first number** after the label.
- Parsing regex (tested 2026-06-15):
  ```python
  import re
  def screener_value(label, html):
      pat = re.compile(
          r'\b' + re.escape(label) + r'\b.*?<span class="nowrap value">(?:\s*₹\s*)?(?:<span class="number">)?([0-9,.\-]+)',
          re.S
      )
      m = pat.search(html)
      return float(m.group(1).replace(',', '')) if m else None
  price = screener_value("Current Price", text)
  mcap  = screener_value("Market Cap", text)
  pe    = screener_value("Stock P/E", text)
  ```
- Limitations: Screener.in blocks detailed ratio pages for unregistered users (`/user/quick_ratios/` redirects to register). So debt-equity, profit growth, promoter holding are usually unavailable via curl. Use the fields above plus Yahoo volume for scoring; do not fake missing ratios.
- Reference: `references/screener-fundamental-parsing-recipe.md`

## TradingView Unusual-Volume Tab — Primary Volume Signal (Added 2026-06-17)
The **"Unusual volume"** tab on TradingView's India stocks page is the highest-signal free source for volume anomalies. It sorts by relative volume (Rel Vol) and includes price, change %, volume, market cap, and P/E for each stock.

- **URL:** `https://www.tradingview.com/markets/stocks-india/market-movers-unusual-volume/` (navigate via browser, then click the "Unusual volume" tab — ref ID varies by session)
- **Extraction method:** Use `browser_console` with JS to extract table data as JSON. **Important:** pull the symbol from the first `<a>` inside the first cell, not by regexing the full cell text — TradingView appends company-name fragments that look like additional ticker characters (e.g. `PRIMIND` becomes `PRIMINDPrime Industries Limited`).
  ```javascript
  (function() {
    const results = [];
    document.querySelectorAll('table tbody tr').forEach(row => {
      const cells = row.querySelectorAll('td');
      if (cells.length < 7) return;
      const symbolA = cells[0].querySelector('a');
      const symbol = symbolA ? symbolA.textContent.trim() : '';
      const relVol = parseFloat(cells[1]?.textContent?.trim() || '');
      const priceText = cells[2]?.textContent?.replace('INR','').replace(/\u202f/g,'').trim() || '';
      const price = parseFloat(priceText);
      if (!symbol || isNaN(price)) return;
      const chg = cells[3]?.textContent?.trim();
      const vol = cells[4]?.textContent?.trim();
      const mcap = cells[5]?.textContent?.trim();
      const pe = cells[6]?.textContent?.trim();
      results.push({symbol, relVol: isNaN(relVol)?null:relVol, price, chg, vol, mcapRaw: mcap, peRaw: pe});
    });
    return JSON.stringify(results, null, 2);
  })();
  ```
  **Note:** Column order varies by tab. The "Unusual volume" tab puts **Rel Vol first**, while "Top gainers" puts **Chg% first**. Inspect the table header row to confirm column order before extracting.

- **Browser DOM extraction pitfall (added 2026-07-01):** In headless browser sessions the table body may not be reachable via `document.querySelectorAll('table tbody tr')`, returning an empty list even though the page visually loads. When this happens, fall back to the HTML-JSON extraction recipe in `references/tv-unusual-volume-extraction-2026-07-01.md`. It parses the embedded column-major data directly and gives the same 100 rows as the UI table.

- **Cross-reference workflow (KEY INSIGHT):** The strongest unpriced-catalyst signal is a stock that appears on TradingView's unusual-volume list with **zero NSE corporate announcements in the last 7 days**. This means the volume is driven by information not yet publicly disclosed. In the 2026-06-17 scan, TFL (12.44x rel vol), MADHUCON (8.01x), and HYBRIDFIN (5.45x) all had no NSE filings — these were the top 3 picks.

## Yahoo Finance v8 Rate Limiting (Added 2026-06-17)
Yahoo Finance v8 can return **HTTP 429 (Too Many Requests)** when making rapid sequential calls. Mitigation:
- Insert `time.sleep(2)` between each ticker price check in Python scripts.
- If 429 is hit, wait 5 seconds and retry that ticker.
- The 429 is transient — it does not mean the API is permanently blocked (unlike the 401 on v7 Quote API which is permanent).

## NSE Announcements API — JSON Format Note (Added 2026-06-17)
The NSE corporate-announcements endpoint returns a **bare JSON array** (`[ {...}, {...} ]`), not `{"data": [...]}`. Scripts that call `data.get('data', [])` will fail with `AttributeError: 'list' object has no attribute 'get'`. Parse with `json.loads()` and check `isinstance(data, list)` directly.

**Cron scanner candidate-universe pitfall (added after 2026-06-19 scan):** Do not expand the Yahoo/Screener fetch universe to every high-signal NSE announcement symbol before price/volume filtering. That can time out because the announcement feed has thousands of records. Use TradingView unusual-volume as the primary candidate universe, then cross-reference NSE announcements for catalyst/negative flags; add announcement-only names only after a cheap prefilter proves they also have volume/liquidity.

**Board-meeting catalyst already priced-in pitfall (added after 2026-06-22 scan):** A stock with "Outcome of Board Meeting" NSE filing BUT already up >+20% in the preceding 5 days is often a **priced-in catalyst**, not an unpriced one. Example: HILTON (+21.4% in 5 days before filing) and CALSOFT (+21.4%) both cleared fundamental filters but the move had already happened. For scoring, downgrade `Earnings_catalyst` from 9-10 to 5-6 when 5-day momentum exceeds +15%, and downgrade `Technical_setup` to 0 (anti-pattern #5). The ideal signal is a board outcome with +5% to +15% 5-day momentum, implying awareness but not exhaustion.

**Post-announcement volume-spike timing pitfall (added after 2026-06-22 scan):** A board-meeting outcome filed on Friday (e.g., 19-Jun) followed by a volume spike on Monday (22-Jun) may look like "unpriced volume," but the 5-day momentum window captures the Friday close → Monday close move. Example: GVPTECH filed board outcome Friday 19-Jun, then spiked 10.4x relVol Monday 22-Jun; 5-day momentum was +13.6%, already capturing the catalyst. Score collapses because the "unpriced" window closed over the weekend. **Rule:** For filings on Fri/Sat, shift the "priced-in" check to compare Friday close vs. the close on the filing day itself. If the filing-day move was >+10%, the catalyst is already reflected even if Monday volume is anomalous.

**Intraday-filing end-of-day pricing pitfall (added after 2026-06-23 scan):** A filing released during market hours (e.g., MOHITIND at 13:32 IST on 19-Jun-2026) may not be reflected in the same-day close if the market does not immediately react. MOHITIND's 19-Jun close was ₹22.63, essentially flat; the revaluation happened on the next trading Tuesday (23-Jun) to ₹26.52. Therefore, when a filing is intraday and the same-day close moved < +5%, treat the catalyst as still largely unpriced regardless of where the 5-day momentum print lands. Only downgrade the catalyst as "priced-in" if the same-day close moved > +10% from the pre-filing price or opening print.

**BSE-only ticker Screener.in 404 pitfall (added after 2026-06-22 scan):** Many penny stocks trade only on BSE (not NSE). Screener.in company pages for these tickers return HTTP 404 because the slug matches the NSE symbol, which may not exist. Examples from 2026-06-22 scan: GUJCRED, VISVEN, JAIPAN, PROCLB, SHRINIWAS, DRL, RFSL, VENLONENT, YUNIKM, NATPLY — all 404'd on Screener.in. **Rule:** When Screener.in returns 404, try the `.BO` suffix on Yahoo Finance v8 instead of `.NS`. If the stock validates via Yahoo `.BO`, note "BSE-listed; Screener verification unavailable" and do not fake mcap/PE data. Do not exclude BSE-only candidates automatically, but downgrade `Chatter_novelty` because BSE announcements are harder to verify via API.

**Market-cap parsing pitfall (added after 2026-06-19 scan):** When parsing TradingView market caps like `1.36 B INR`, require the unit before `INR` (or parse a robust unit-aware pattern). A loose optional-unit regex can parse `1.36 B INR` as `1.36e-7 Cr` and falsely pass the mcap filter.

## Bot Detection Mitigation
1. **Add delays:** Insert `sleep(2-4s)` between rapid browser or curl calls
2. **Rotate User-Agent:** Use a recent Chrome desktop string for curl scripts
3. **Avoid sorting/filtering on Screener.in** — these actions frequently redirect to login/register; use pre-built screen URLs instead
4. **If a site blocks the browser stack entirely** (e.g., BSE / NSE "Access Denied"), immediately fall back to TradingView or Google Finance rather than retrying the same URL. For BSE-only candidates, if BSE filings are blocked, keep the name only if volume/fundamental filters still pass and explicitly disclose "BSE filing verification blocked" as a risk.

## Scorer Bugs Fixed (2026-07-20)
