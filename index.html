The Stack

Six modules. One desk. Zero missed inputs.

Every scheduled agent above belongs to one of these modules. Each is built with the funding and ALM reality of an NBFC-ML lender in mind — 15–20 bp of avoidable funding cost compounds into crores of P&L drag over a year.

🌅 Morning Open

Pre-market intelligence by 07:45 IST.

  • Indices, FII/DII flows, USD/INR
  • Global overnight: US, Europe, Asia, oil
  • Verified RBI rates & liquidity ops
  • G-Sec yields, OIS curve, CP/NCD levels

🚨 RBI Intelligence

Every press release and circular — parsed, deduped, delivered with NBFC impact notes.

  • Flash alerts on release
  • Master Directions, SBR, FEMA
  • Impact notes for a secured retail/MSME book
  • PAST → PRESENT → FUTURE framing

💰 Funding Optimization

Borrowing mix vs live market prices, daily.

  • Bank TL vs NCD vs CP vs ECB cost comparison
  • CP calendar & rollover alerts
  • Concentration & tenor mismatch flags
  • ECB all-in cost vs domestic arbitrage

💧 Liquidity Pulse

Systemic + structural liquidity, monitored continuously.

  • LAF/VRR results & system surplus
  • Call money & repo rates
  • 30-day liquidity buffer tracking
  • ALM gap alerts (0–1M, 1–3M buckets)

📊 T-Bill & G-Sec Monitor

Auction intelligence with cut-off yield tracking.

  • 91/182/364-day T-bill cut-offs
  • G-Sec auction calendar & cut-offs
  • OIS curve & spread monitoring
  • Sovereign yield signals for pricing

🌙 EOD & Overnight Risk

Close-of-day recap + pre-sleep risk scan.

  • Full day recap: rates, FX, credit, oil
  • Overnight risk flags before Asia opens
  • Position risk assessment
  • Next-day calendar & auction preview

Desk Impact

What this means for the Hero FinCorp balance sheet.

The stack is not a dashboard — it is the desk. Every module maps to a line item the treasury owns.

Funding cost

Daily CP vs NCD vs bank-line repricing catches 15–20 bp of drift before it compounds. On a ₹30,000 Cr borrow book, 10 bp ≈ ₹30 Cr/year of interest cost.

ALM & liquidity

Liquidity pulse + buffer tracking surfaces 0–1M and 1–3M gaps before they hit the ALM statement. Regulatory liquidity coverage is a moat, not an afterthought.

Regulatory radar

RBI Master Directions, scale-based regulations, and FEMA changes reach the desk within 30 minutes — not a week after the comment deadline.

IPO-readiness

With a ₹3,408 Cr IPO in the pipeline (dates TBA), the desk runs on the same discipline a listed treasury will face: mark-to-market, disclosure-grade data, zero missed events.
