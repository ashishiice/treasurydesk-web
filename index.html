The Problem

Your funding cost is decided by information you don't have.

Mid-sized NBFCs (₹200–2,000 Cr AUM) face the same treasury complexity as large banks — but without the headcount. Every day, decisions are made on stale rates, missed circulars, and gut feel.

RBI circulars move your ALM

New Master Directions, scale-based regulations, liquidity norms — you read about them in the newspaper a week after the deadline to comment.

Funding costs drift silently

Borrowing mix, CP vs NCD vs bank lines — 15–20 bps of avoidable cost compounds into crores of P&L drag over a year.

Liquidity is managed reactively

You find out you're short only when the lender calls. Structural liquidity gaps show up in ALM statements — after the quarter ends.

A full desk is unaffordable

A competent treasury team costs ₹60L+ a year. A fractional consultant costs ₹25–40L and still can't watch the market 24/7.
